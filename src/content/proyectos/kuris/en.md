Kuris is a ground drone designed to move over any terrain. Instead of propellers, it stands on three curved arms that end in spherical wheels, so it can move in any direction and cross uneven ground without losing its balance, and all the character of the product sits in a domed head with a single green light eye.

In the 3D viewer above you can turn the drone around, zoom in on the arms and wheels, change the finish (bronze, white or sand) and switch the light of the eye and the ring off or on. In the "On the move" view, Kuris loops around a track and climbs over a bump to show how it handles uneven ground.

## The idea

I wanted Kuris to read as a sturdy vehicle and, at the same time, as a small character, since that is what makes a product like this memorable. That is why the shape is very simple (a dome, a disc and three arms) and the expression comes from the eye: an elongated slot of green light that works both as a gaze and as a status indicator.

## The shape

The body is a dome resting on a disc surrounded by a white light ring, which outlines the drone in low light. Three arms leave the disc at 120°, curving downwards and tapering to the wheel like legs. With three contact points, the drone always rests on all three wheels even when the ground is not flat, and the rubber spherical wheels let it turn on the spot.
